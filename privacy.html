Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

This policy covers Pilot Funds Uploader ("the app"), a private tool operated by the owner of the Pilot Funds YouTube channel.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by the Pilot Funds channel owner, to manage the Pilot Funds channel. It is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests these YouTube permissions:

  • youtube.upload, to upload videos and thumbnails to the Pilot Funds channel.
  • youtube.readonly, to read the channel's own ID and title so uploads go to the right channel.

The app also reads public YouTube data (video titles, view counts, channel subscriber counts) through the YouTube Data API for topic research.

How the data is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database. It does not collect, sell, share or transfer any personal data to anyone.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app from uploading. Data stored by the app can be deleted by deleting the local token file.
