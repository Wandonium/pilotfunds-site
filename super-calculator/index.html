General information only. This calculator doesn't consider your personal objectives, financial situation or needs, and it isn't financial advice. Projections are estimates based on the assumptions shown. Real returns go up and down, and fees, insurance and rule changes will change the result. The take-home figure is an estimate using 2026–27 tax rates plus the 2% Medicare levy, and ignores offsets and Division 293 tax. Consider talking to a licensed financial adviser before making decisions about your super.

Sources: ATO Taxation Statistics 2023–24 (median super balance by age and sex; averages computed from Individuals table 23A). ASFA Retirement Standard, June quarter 2026 (comfortable lump sum: $630,000 single, $730,000 couple). Super Consumers Australia retirement savings targets (medium spending: $322,000 single, $432,000 couple). Both targets assume you own your home and get a part Age Pension. Rules: super guarantee 12%, contributions tax 15%, concessional cap $32,500 for 2026–27.
